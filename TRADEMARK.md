# Trademark policy

**creagent**, the creagent wordmark, the pixel mascot and the logo mark are
trademarks of manudev. They are *not* covered by the licence in
[LICENSE](LICENSE) — the FSL grants rights to the code, and says so explicitly
under "Trademarks".

That distinction is the point of this file. The code may be read, run, forked
and modified inside what the licence permits; the name is what tells someone
they are talking to us. A fork that keeps the name is not a fork, it is a
lookalike, and the people it confuses are the ones who trusted the name.

## You may

- say your project is built on, forked from or compatible with creagent
- keep the copyright notices and licence text the licence requires you to keep
- link to https://creagent.fun

## You may not, without written permission

- name your fork, service, product, app or company creagent, or anything
  close enough to be mistaken for it
- use the logo mark, the wordmark or the mascot as the identity of your own
  project — including as a favicon, app icon or social avatar
- register creagent, or a confusingly similar name, as a domain, package name,
  app-store listing, social handle or trademark
- imply that we endorse, sponsor or maintain your fork
